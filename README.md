# Shop.com Marketing Performance Analysis & Business Recommendations
## Business Problem

Shop.com, an online retail business, is experiencing declining customer engagement and conversion rates despite launching multiple digital marketing campaigns.

The business seeks to understand the factors influencing marketing effectiveness, customer interactions, and purchasing behaviour.

### Key Business Challenges

- **Reduced Customer Engagement:** Declining interactions
  with marketing content and the online platform.
- **Decreased Conversion Rates:** Fewer visitors are
  progressing to completed purchases.
- **Marketing Effectiveness:** Evaluating whether marketing
  activities generate meaningful business outcomes.
- **Customer Feedback:** Understanding customer opinions,
  product ratings, and sentiment to improve customer experience.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Shop.com's marketing performance from 2023 to 2025 to evaluate customer engagement, content effectiveness, product performance, and conversion trends.

Using data analytics and business intelligence techniques, the project aims to identify marketing performance gaps, understand customer behaviour, and transform analytical findings into actionable business insights that support marketing optimization and data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate marketing performance across key business metrics.
- Analyze customer engagement through views, clicks, and likes.
- Examine conversion trends and identify opportunities to improve purchase conversion.
- Assess product performance and customer ratings.
- Analyze customer sentiment and feedback to understand customer perceptions.
- Identify content and marketing strategies that drive stronger customer engagement.
- Develop recommendations to improve marketing effectiveness and business performance.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Views:** Measures marketing content reach and visibility.
- **Total Clicks:** Evaluates customer interaction with marketing content.
- **Total Likes:** Measures audience engagement and content appreciation.
- **Conversion Rate:** Evaluates the effectiveness of converting customer interactions into purchases.
- **Product Ratings:** Measures customer satisfaction through product reviews.
- **Customer Sentiment:** Evaluates customer opinions and perceptions through feedback analysis.

## Tools and Technologies

- **SQL / T-SQL:** Data querying, transformation, and analytical preparation.
- **Python:** Data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and customer sentiment analysis.
- **Pandas / NumPy:** Data manipulation and numerical analysis.
- **VADER:** Natural language processing and sentiment classification.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, KPI development, and business intelligence reporting.
- **DAX:** Calculated measures and marketing performance metrics.
- **MS PowerPoint:** Executive data storytelling and stakeholder presentation.

## Project Structure

- `data/` — Dataset storage and data documentation.
- `notebooks/` — Jupyter notebooks for exploratory data analysis, analytical investigations, sentiment analysis.
- `sql/` — SQL scripts for data querying, transformation, and analysis.
- `powerbi/` — Power BI reports and dashboard documentation in pbix and PDF formats.
- `images/` — All dashboard screenshots and project visuals.

## Key Findings

- **Marketing Reach:** Over 9 million views recorded across the analyzed marketing activities.
- **Customer Engagement:** More than 1.78 million clicks and 414,000 likes recorded.
- **Conversion Performance:** Conversion rate declined from 11.58% in 2023 to 8.55% in 2025.
- **Customer Satisfaction:** Average product rating of 3.69 out of 5.

## Expected Business Value

The findings and recommendations are intended to support better marketing decisions, improve customer experiences, optimize campaign strategies, and strengthen overall business performance.
